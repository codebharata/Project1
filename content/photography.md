# WEDDING PHOTOGRAPHY SERVICES

## We help people better their wedding experience with great Videography and Photography Services

We travel across the country creating an incredible montage of the most priceless moments of the weddings we cover. We have team of 15+ skilled photographers and Videographers.

Together with the essential formal portraits of your wedding party, family and friends, you will receive a complete collection of images capturing the signature moments of your day.

Enjoy your day knowing that your wedding story will be documented as an artistic statement as totally unique as you! We are fortunate to count many of our clients as lifelong friends and look forward to each wedding as a privilege and honor as being part of one the most important days of your lives.

While candid wedding photography is our forte, we click-happy charm to save-the-date, engagement, pre and post wedding shoots and of course, traditional photography.

We also cover wedding video in Traditional and cinematics with multi angles.

We also photograph proposals, bar and bat mitzvahs, special events, and outdoor family portraits. Please contact us for pricing and additional information on these services.

We will respond to your inquiry via email and/or a phone call within 24 hours unless photographing a wedding or on holiday. Then, it may take an additional day to get back to you.

We offer specially designed packages for every need and budget. We will show sample pictures and video highlights during a meeting. We also provide wedding photo albums
